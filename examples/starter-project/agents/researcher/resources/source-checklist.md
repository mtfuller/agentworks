# Source Quality Checklist

Before citing a source, check:

- **Recency** -- is it still current for a fast-moving topic?
- **Primary vs. secondary** -- is this the original source, or someone
  summarizing it (and possibly introducing errors)?
- **Author/publisher credibility** -- do they have relevant expertise, and
  any conflict of interest?
- **Corroboration** -- does at least one independent source agree?

If a claim fails more than one of these, flag it as uncertain in the
synthesis rather than stating it plainly.
